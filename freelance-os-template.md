# SistemFlow — Freelance OS

## Overview
This is the core product of the SistemFlow suite. It is designed for freelancers, consultants and small digital businesses that need a single workspace to manage clients, projects, income, follow-up and work planning.

## FAQ
### Does it include Notion database templates?
Yes. It is structured around reusable databases and views.

### Can it be customized?
Yes. Each database and page can be edited without limitation.

### Is it designed for scaling?
Yes. It can evolve into a more advanced business dashboard or agency workflow later.
